# Cosmological Parameters Explanation (Including Modified Gravity)

| **Parameter**    | **Formula**                                | **Physical Meaning**                                                                                      | **Units**         | **Relation to other parameters**                                  | **Context/Importance**                                                                 |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Ωₘ** (Omega_m) | -                                          | Fraction of the total energy density of the universe contributed by matter (both dark and ordinary matter). | -                | Related to **Ω_b** and **Ω_cold**.                                   | Key for determining the total matter content of the universe.                           |
| **Ω_b** (Omega_b)| -                                          | Fraction of the total energy density contributed by baryons (ordinary matter).                             | -                | A subcomponent of **Ωₘ**.                                             | Important for understanding the role of regular matter in the evolution of the universe.|
| **nₛ** (n_s)     | -                                          | Scalar spectral index, describing the slope of the primordial power spectrum of density fluctuations.      | -                | Affects the power spectrum, related to **A_s**.                       | Determines the distribution of cosmic structure in the early universe.                  |
| **Aₛ** (A_s)     | -                                          | Amplitude of the primordial curvature power spectrum, related to the strength of initial density fluctuations.| -                | Influenced by **n_s** and **σ₈**.                                      | Helps describe the initial conditions of the universe and large-scale structure.        |
| **h** (Hubble)   | -                                          | Hubble parameter, which defines the rate of expansion of the universe at present time, in units of 100 km/s/Mpc. | km/s/Mpc         | Important in determining the age of the universe.                      | Fundamental for defining the current expansion rate of the universe.                   |
| **σ₈** (sigma8)  | -                                          | Amplitude of the matter power spectrum on a scale of 8 Mpc/h, representing the level of structure formation. | -                | Related to **Ωₘ** and **A_s**.                                          | Sets the scale for the distribution of matter in the universe.                         |
| **aₑₓₚ** (expfactor) | a(t) / a₀                                  | The scale factor of the universe, describing its expansion over time.                                         | -                | Related to **Ωₘ** and **ΩΛ**.                                           | Key for understanding the evolution of the universe’s size over time.                   |
| **k** (wavenumber) | k [h Mpc⁻¹]                               | Wave number associated with the Fourier modes of the cosmic density fluctuations, in units of h Mpc⁻¹.      | h Mpc⁻¹          | Linked to the primordial power spectrum.                             | Describes the fluctuations in the density of the universe.                             |
| **omega_cold**   | -                                          | Fraction of the total energy density contributed by cold dark matter.                                       | -                | Part of **Ωₘ**, used alongside **Ω_b** to estimate total matter.      | Crucial for understanding dark matter’s contribution to the structure of the universe.  |
| **sigma8_cold**  | -                                          | The value of the matter power spectrum at a scale of 8 Mpc/h for cold dark matter.                          | -                | Directly influences the structure formation in the universe.         | Determines the clustering of cold dark matter over large scales.                       |
| **omega_baryon** | -                                          | Fraction of the total energy density contributed by baryons (ordinary matter).                             | -                | Directly affects **Ω_b** and **Ωₘ**.                                    | Important for understanding the role of baryons in cosmic evolution.                   |
| **ns**           | -                                          | Same as **nₛ** above.                                                                                       | -                | -                                                                 | -                                                                                      |
| **hubble**       | -                                          | Same as **h** above.                                                                                        | km/s/Mpc         | -                                                                 | -                                                                                      |
| **neutrino_mass**| -                                          | The sum of the masses of the neutrinos, influencing the early universe's evolution.                          | eV               | Affects the expansion rate and structure formation in the early universe. | Plays a role in cosmic evolution and structure formation at early times.               |
| **w₀**           | -                                          | The present value of the equation of state parameter for dark energy.                                       | -                | Part of the parameterization for dark energy equation of state **w(a)**. | Defines the current state of dark energy in the universe.                             |
| **wₐ**           | -                                          | The variation in the equation of state parameter for dark energy over time.                                  | -                | Together with **w₀**, forms the **w(a)** equation for dark energy.     | Important for understanding the evolution of dark energy over time.                    |

## Modified Gravity Parameters

| **Parameter**    | **Formula**                                | **Physical Meaning**                                                                                      | **Units**         | **Relation to other parameters**                                  | **Context/Importance**                                                                 |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **f(R)**         | f(R) = R + αR^2 + ...                      | A modification of the Einstein-Hilbert action that introduces a function of Ricci scalar, altering the gravitational dynamics. | -                | Affects the gravitational interactions, related to **Ωₘ** and **ΩΛ**. | Used in modified gravity theories to explain cosmic acceleration without invoking dark energy. |
| **f₁** (Scalar Field) | -                                      | Represents the coupling function of scalar fields in scalar-tensor theories of gravity.                     | -                | Related to **α** and other scalar field parameters.                | Influences the behavior of the scalar field in the modified gravitational theory.        |
| **β**            | -                                          | Coupling strength between the scalar field and matter.                                                       | -                | Impacts **f(R)** models, affecting structure formation and cosmological expansion. | Key in modified theories where scalar fields interact with matter.                      |
| **α** (f(R) Gravity) | αR²                                   | Represents the parameter controlling the deviation from general relativity in f(R) gravity theories.         | -                | Related to cosmological parameters such as **Ωₘ** and **ΩΛ**.      | Important for models attempting to explain cosmic acceleration by modifying gravity.     |
| **γ** (Chameleon Field) | -                                      | Describes the field that can vary in strength depending on the environment, especially in cosmological structures. | -                | Affects structure formation and cosmological models.                | Key for explaining how modified gravity effects are suppressed in high-density regions.  |
| **β** (f(R) Coupling) | β = f'(R) / R                          | Defines the coupling between scalar field and curvature in f(R) gravity models.                             | -                | Affects structure formation and cosmological dynamics.             | Important for modifying gravitational interactions in different regimes.                |
